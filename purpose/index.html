Our purpose / What we care about

WE ARE HERE TO BUILD.

The Sanctum Eros Leadership Committee was founded to strengthen how Sanctum Eros is managed, operated, and developed—and to create more education, resources, and meaningful collaboration for the wider kink community. Sanctum Eros is an independent, queer-owned 18+ kink organization; its private dungeon play events are 21+.

01 / Why SELC exists

MORE THAN A VENUE.

A kink organization needs leadership that works beyond event night: coordinating volunteers, improving operations, hearing feedback, developing educational opportunities, and addressing practical problems. Stronger community resources also come from sharing knowledge and learning with people outside our own organization.

SELC was established to give that work structure, continuity, and more than one perspective. It is part of Sanctum Eros—not a separate club or a replacement for the Founder. The committee has five Advisor positions, three Representative positions and two Coordinator positions, with Eros serving as Founder/Director. Members can contribute in person and online, and the committee's educational and community partnerships extend beyond Sanctum Eros.

The goal is to help Sanctum Eros function well while also building something useful for the wider kink community: practical education, accessible resources, organizer collaboration, and opportunities for people to contribute. That requires listening, following through, and continually improving.

02 / What we care about

PRINCIPLES THAT SHOW UP.

Consent

Clear, informed, ongoing.

Consent matters during conversation, negotiation, demonstrations, play, and everyday community interaction. It can be withdrawn. No title, reputation, authority, or relationship overrides another person's boundaries.

Education

Skill without gatekeeping.

Practical teaching should explain not only what to do, but what can go wrong, what to watch for, and when to stop. Beginners deserve good information; experienced kinksters should remain curious and accountable.

Community

Belonging beyond a scene.

Conversation, friendship, volunteering, accessible resources, and ordinary social connection matter. Being here should not require having a play partner, performing a role, or participating sexually.

Privacy

Trust is not publicity.

We treat members' identities, experiences, attendance, and sensitive concerns as information entrusted to us. Private venue details and incident records are restricted; not every leader needs to see everything.

Accountability

Take concerns seriously.

A concern deserves a way to be heard without rumor or retaliation. Staff reports, IA investigations, and Ban Reports are different processes with documented authority, review, and opportunities to address conflicts of interest.

Accessibility

Make room for real people.

We work to reduce barriers around disability, experience, finances, scheduling, and online participation. Access needs are part of event and education planning, not an inconvenience added afterward.

03 / How we lead

AUTHORITY WITH RESPONSIBILITY.

SELC Representatives contribute to education, committee proposals, event operations, outreach, and community projects. Coordinators occupy separate leadership positions and help keep that work organized and moving. Staff and Dungeon Monitors still have meaningful operational authority, but they are not automatically SELC members. A separate SELC application and Founder acceptance are required, followed by SELC certification before a role becomes active. Specialist Internal Affairs duties require their own separate approval and certification.

Leadership is not a shortcut around consent or privacy. We distinguish an incident report from a Ban Report; we document decisions; and we require independent review when a decision-maker has a conflict. We do not equate an allegation with a finding. When a pending Ban Report places admission on hold, the restriction should have a defined review path rather than disappearing into an inbox.

SELC also works to connect with other organizations and educators. Community Leaders can apply to collaborate on education, shared resources, practical organizing, and, under controlled conditions, safety information. No external organization receives our membership database or private case files automatically.

Our aim is neither perfection nor the appearance of perfection. It is to create processes people can understand, question, improve, and rely on.

04 / Who can participate

YOU DO NOT NEED TO KNOW EVERYTHING.

You can contribute by teaching, volunteering, reviewing ideas, offering feedback, or helping others feel welcome. Some work happens at events; some happens online. Curiosity and follow-through count.

Age and attendance: Sanctum Eros is an 18+ kink organization. Party events require 21+, while social events are 18+ even when hosted at the dungeon. Individual event requirements may vary. The dungeon is alcohol-free.
