Our role

SUPPORTING SANCTUM EROS.

SELC brings together members who want to give something back to Sanctum Eros. We plan events, support volunteers, welcome new members and help shape what comes next. The Hub explains the wider organization and its mission; SELC explains how the leadership work gets done.

01 / Responsibilities

FOCUS ON WHAT MATTERS.

Before an event, we're reviewing applications, coordinating the team and getting everything ready. Afterward, we listen to feedback and look for ways to make the next event better.

SELC is limited to internal Sanctum Eros management. The Founder/Director directs the organization; two Coordinators oversee administration; four Representatives carry out authorized membership and event operations; and two Advisors provide input and participate in eligible discussions. Staff and Dungeon Monitors have separately defined venue duties.

02 / Standards

THE BASICS, DONE WELL.

Consent

Boundaries are explicit, informed, ongoing and revocable at any time.

Privacy

Treat member information and private conversations with discretion.

Accountability

Concerns are recorded and reviewed fairly. An allegation is not automatically a finding.

Consistency

We publish events we can deliver and give clear updates when plans change.

Accessibility

Members can raise practical access needs before attending.

Good judgment

Leadership roles come with responsibility, not exemption from the rules.

03 / How work moves

ONE REQUEST. A CLEAR NEXT STEP.

Membership applications

Authorized Representatives and Coordinators review the complete application, may ask for clarification and document the outcome. Advisors cannot approve or decline membership.

Events and RSVPs

Operational teams coordinate capacity, volunteers, check-in and questions about participation. Final decisions and publication stay with their designated approving roles.

Messages and safety

Member-authorized messages and feedback can be reviewed in SELC. Incident reports and enforcement matters remain in restricted channels with appropriate access and review.

04 / Responsibilities

AUTHORITY IS NOT INTERCHANGEABLE.

Different roles have different authority. SE membership, an SELC appointment, and a Staff or Dungeon Monitor assignment are not substitutes for one another.

Member decisions

Authorized Representatives and Coordinators review applications and documented clarifications. Advisors may provide eligible feedback but do not approve or decline membership.

Organizational changes

Eligible leadership members participate in designated proposals and votes. The applicable authorization determines who can publish a change or carry out a decision.

Safety and accountability

Immediate venue concerns go to on-duty Staff or Dungeon Monitors. Confidential incident review, restrictions, and appeals remain in the restricted Portal process, not the public SELC website.

05 / Attending

START WITH AN EVENT.

Sanctum Eros membership is free for adults 18+. Dungeon Nights are 21+ and require approved SE membership; non-member admission is not offered. At the dungeon, only educational events and Meet the Dungeon may be opened to non-members. Other events require approved membership. Kinky Game Nights are 21+. The current admission amount, age limits and other requirements are specified in the event listing rather than duplicated here. The dungeon is alcohol-free.

Attending a social or workshop does not replace the Dungeon Night orientation requirement or waive the separate $10 Dungeon Night orientation fee for first-time attendees.

To attend, choose an event, review the rules, and RSVP through the Portal. You do not need to join SELC.

Membership and event access

Free membership remains the foundation of Sanctum Eros. Devotee, Patron and Keyholder are optional paid memberships. The Sanctum Passport is prepaid admission for eligible Dungeon Nights. Neither gives anyone additional leadership authority.

At the dungeon, only educational events and Meet the Dungeon may be opened to non-members. All other events require approved membership.

Payment verification and donations are handled through the Owner Dashboard. SELC continues to review membership and event requests within its existing responsibilities.
