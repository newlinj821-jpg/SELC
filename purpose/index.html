Our role

SUPPORTING SANCTUM EROS.

SELC exists to support Sanctum Eros and its events. We coordinate practical work so members know what to expect, staff know what to do, and concerns receive proper attention.

See upcoming eventsApply for membership

01 / Responsibilities

FOCUS ON WHAT MATTERS.

Our work is reviewing member applications, handling RSVPs and ticket issues, preparing venues and staff, responding to member feedback, and making thoughtful decisions about events.

We work on Sanctum Eros operations. The Founder directs the organization; Coordinators, Representatives, Advisors, Staff, and Dungeon Monitors have defined responsibilities.

02 / Standards

THE BASICS, DONE WELL.

Consent

Boundaries are explicit, informed, ongoing and revocable at any time.

Privacy

Attendance, member information, reports and private venue details are shared only as operationally appropriate.

Accountability

Concerns are recorded and reviewed fairly. An allegation is not automatically a finding.

Consistency

We publish events we can deliver and give clear updates when plans change.

Accessibility

Members can raise practical access needs before attending.

Good judgment

Leadership roles come with responsibility, not exemption from the rules.

03 / Attending

START WITH AN EVENT.

Sanctum Eros membership is free and available to adults 18+. Dungeon Nights are 21+ and require approved membership. Socials and workshops follow their individual posted eligibility and orientation rules. The dungeon is alcohol-free.

To attend, choose an event, review the rules, and RSVP through the Portal. You do not need to join SELC.
