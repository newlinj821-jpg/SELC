SELC / Cross-organization collaboration

BETTER COMMUNITIES TOGETHER.

Community Leaders is a reviewed network for owners and authorized organizers of other adult kink and BDSM organizations. Share resources, ask for advice, collaborate on education, and work through practical questions with people who understand what it takes to run a community.

Why this network exists

NO ORGANIZATION HAS TO KNOW EVERYTHING.

Shared education

Trade workshop outlines, develop accessible material, connect educators with venues, and support new instructors with practical mentorship.

Organizing experience

Talk through staffing, volunteer training, event design, attendee expectations, incident procedures, equipment checks, and the administrative work behind safe events.

Responsible safety coordination

Discuss procedures and, where necessary, request carefully limited inter-organizational safety notices. Consent, need-to-know access, independent review, and privacy take precedence over gossip.

Community Leaders are not SELC Representatives and do not receive Sanctum Eros Staff, IA, RSVP, ticket, membership, or venue-control permissions. An approval only opens the specific collaboration tools they need.

Confidential information

TRUST IS EARNED.

A cross-organization relationship is not permission to circulate someone’s personal information. We keep reports of incidents, Ban Reports, and formal IA investigations distinct. There is no automatic exchange of raw IA records, identity documents, member lists, or private event addresses.

Any proposed safety notice goes through a separate decision process. SELC must establish a legitimate reason, minimize the information, document consent or another appropriate basis for sharing, and obtain final Founder authorization. Approved recipients must treat information as confidential, factual, and context-dependent; allegations are not findings.

Community Leaders is for people committed to improving community practices, not a shared blacklist or an alternative to consent and due process.
