SELC / Cross-organization collaboration

BETTER COMMUNITIES TOGETHER.

Community Leaders is a reviewed network for owners and authorized organizers of other adult kink and BDSM organizations. Share resources, ask for advice, collaborate on education, and work through practical questions with people who understand what it takes to run a community.

Why this network exists

NO ORGANIZATION HAS TO KNOW EVERYTHING.

Shared education

Trade workshop outlines, develop accessible material, connect educators with venues, and support new instructors with practical mentorship.

Organizing experience

Talk through staffing, volunteer training, event design, attendee expectations, incident procedures, equipment checks, and the administrative work behind safe events.

Responsible safety coordination

Discuss procedures and, where necessary, request carefully limited inter-organizational safety notices. Consent, need-to-know access, independent review, and privacy take precedence over gossip.

Community Leaders are not SELC Representatives or committee voters. They do not receive Sanctum Eros Staff, IA, RSVP, ticket, membership, or venue-control permissions. SELC appointments require approved SE membership, separate Founder acceptance, and SELC certification. An approval only opens the specific collaboration tools they need.

Confidential information

TRUST IS EARNED.

A cross-organization relationship is not permission to circulate someone’s personal information. We keep reports of incidents, Ban Reports, and formal IA investigations distinct. There is no automatic exchange of raw IA records, identity documents, member lists, or private event addresses.

Any proposed safety notice goes through a separate decision process. SELC must establish a legitimate reason, minimize the information, document consent or another appropriate basis for sharing, and obtain final Founder authorization. Approved recipients must treat information as confidential, factual, and context-dependent; allegations are not findings.

Community Leaders is for people committed to improving community practices, not a shared blacklist or an alternative to consent and due process.

Application / Verified organizer accounts

YOUR ORGANIZATION. YOUR ACCOUNT.

Community Leaders is independent of Sanctum Eros membership. You do not need to join Sanctum Eros to participate. Create a verified organizer account, complete an application, and wait for Founder approval. Your login uses the same secure Portal infrastructure, password reset, and email service.

Do you already have a Sanctum Eros account?

Choose your existing SE account or set up a Community Leaders-only organizer account. Both options lead to the same application.

Use your existing SE account

Your existing Sanctum Eros username or verified email works here. No duplicate account is created. Community Leaders approval is separate from SE membership and committee appointment.

Create your Community Leaders account

You do not need Sanctum Eros membership. This verified account uses the Portal’s existing email and password-reset services; it does not give access to Staff, tickets, private IA cases, or SELC committee controls.

Verify your email

Enter the code sent to your email. The code expires after 20 minutes. After verification, sign in and complete the application.

Reset your password

Use the existing Sanctum Eros Portal email-reset system. A reset code is sent to the verified email on your account.

Your application

Sign in with a verified account to continue. Only the Founder can approve or revoke Community Leaders access.

After approval

YOUR NETWORK WORKSPACE.

Approved leaders can message the Founder, SELC, or each other, share organizing resources, and submit safety notices for controlled review. No external report automatically becomes a Sanctum Eros ban or finding.
