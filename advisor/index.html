Become an Advisor and help build education, outreach, community resources, and meaningful collaboration across the wider kink community. Sanctum Eros membership is not required to become an Advisor.

What Advisors do

  • Contribute to education, outreach, networking, and community initiatives
  • Discuss and vote on eligible SELC proposals
  • Bring perspectives from beyond Sanctum Eros
  • Participate remotely or in person as appropriate

Five Advisor positions. Applicants must be 18+, complete an application, receive Founder approval, and pass the Advisor certification. An Advisor position does not provide SE membership, event admission, Staff access, or access to confidential investigations.

Want to become a Representative later?

Advisors can apply separately for free Sanctum Eros membership. If approved, they can then seek Representative advancement and complete its separate approval and certification.
