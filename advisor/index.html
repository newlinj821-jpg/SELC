Sanctum Eros Advisors help the organization listen to members, improve events, and make sounder decisions. This is a genuine committee position, with responsibilities and boundaries, but not the administrative workload of a Representative or Coordinator.

01 / The role

LISTEN, PARTICIPATE, CONTRIBUTE.

Advisors work with the Founder, Coordinators, and Representatives to identify improvements in the everyday experience of Sanctum Eros members. Your perspective is valuable precisely because you do not need to manage every event to make it better.

Advisors can review member communications explicitly shared with SELC, consider suggestions and event feedback, join internal discussions, and participate in eligible committee proposals. Your input can help shape event operations, accessibility, communication, and member support.

The private SELC chat is available to appointed and certified Advisors, as well as Representatives, Coordinators, and the Founder. It is not a public discussion channel.

02 / Responsibilities and boundaries

THE RIGHT AUTHORITY.

Member feedback

Listen carefully and contribute to responses to messages that members have authorized SELC to review.

Event improvement

Bring constructive ideas on scheduling, hospitality, accessibility, communication, and the overall member experience.

Committee proposals

Discuss proposals, challenge ideas respectfully, and vote where your Advisor role is eligible.

Confidentiality

Respect private correspondence and keep committee material within approved channels.

Escalation

Refer incident reports and urgent safety concerns to authorized Representatives, Coordinators, staff, or the Founder.

Clear limits

Advisors do not independently approve applications, change member access, review restricted IA case files, or issue bans.

03 / Eligibility

WHO SHOULD APPLY?

Applicants must be at least 18, have approved and active Sanctum Eros membership, and be willing to work respectfully with other volunteers. Experience in kink organizations is useful, but a practical perspective, reliability, confidentiality, and a willingness to hear disagreement are equally important.

SELC is an internal management committee. It is not an outside organization or networking program. Advisor participation does not replace event staffing or venue qualifications. Dungeon Nights remain 21+ even though organizational membership is 18+.

04 / Applying

AN APPLICATION. NOT AN AUTOMATIC APPOINTMENT.

1. Have SE membership

Apply for or confirm your approved member account. Membership is free.

2. Sign in to SELC

Use the same member credentials you use for the Portal and select the Advisor application.

3. Complete the review

Answer the role-specific questions. The Founder reviews applications, and approved candidates complete certification before appointment.
