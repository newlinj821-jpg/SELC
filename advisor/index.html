01 / The role

LISTEN, PARTICIPATE, CONTRIBUTE.

Work alongside the rest of the committee, bring forward ideas and help us see things from different perspectives.

Advisors can review member communications shared with SELC, consider suggestions and event feedback, use the private committee chat, and introduce or discuss proposals within their assigned permissions. Their input may improve event preparation, accessibility, communication and the overall member experience.

The private SELC chat is available to appointed and certified Advisors, as well as Representatives, Coordinators, and the Founder. It is not a public discussion channel.

02 / Responsibilities and boundaries

THE RIGHT AUTHORITY.

Member feedback

Listen carefully and contribute to responses to messages that members have authorized SELC to review.

Event improvement

Bring constructive ideas on scheduling, hospitality, accessibility, communication, and the overall member experience.

Committee proposals

Discuss proposals, challenge ideas respectfully, and vote where your Advisor role is eligible.

Confidentiality

Respect private correspondence and keep committee material within approved channels.

Escalation

Refer incident reports and urgent safety concerns to authorized Representatives, Coordinators, staff, or the Founder.

Clear limits

Advisors do not independently approve applications, change member access, review restricted IA case files, or issue bans.

03 / Eligibility

WHO SHOULD APPLY?

Applicants must be at least 18, have an approved and active Sanctum Eros membership and use their existing member login to apply. You do not need extensive organizational experience. We value reliability, sound judgment, confidentiality, the ability to listen when others disagree and the willingness to make a useful contribution rather than simply hold a title.

Advisors are volunteers in an internal SE committee, not an independent governing body. The Hub explains where SELC fits in the wider organization. Advisors do not gain automatic access to restricted safety files, the ability to admit guests or the authority to change venue rules. Events at the dungeon require approved membership except educational events and Meet the Dungeon when explicitly opened to non-members. Dungeon Nights and Kinky Game Nights remain 21+.

04 / Applying

AN APPLICATION. NOT AN AUTOMATIC APPOINTMENT.

1. Have SE membership

Apply for or confirm your approved member account. Membership is free.

2. Sign in to SELC

Use the same member credentials you use for the Portal and select the Advisor application.

3. Complete the review

Answer the role-specific questions. The Founder reviews applications, and approved candidates complete certification before appointment.

Start Advisor applicationMy member account
How approval works

Submitting an application does not grant committee access. The Founder reviews the application, may ask for clarification and, if accepted, assigns Advisor certification. Access begins after the appointment and required certification are completed. Application history remains available in your account.

Admission to SE events and appointment to SELC are separate. Existing accounts and past applications remain on record.

05 / Common questions

BEFORE YOU APPLY.

Do I have to be an existing Sanctum Eros member?

Yes. Advisor applications are for approved, active SE members. Membership is free and separate from appointment to SELC.

Is this the same as working as event Staff or a Dungeon Monitor?

No. Advisor service does not authorize venue supervision, demonstrations, member admission or incident enforcement. Those duties require the appropriate staff appointment and training.

Can an Advisor read member applications or decide who is admitted?

No. Membership applications and approval controls are restricted to authorized Representatives, Coordinators and the Founder. Advisors can help with feedback and member-authorized communications but not independent approvals.

How do I apply if I already have a member account?

Choose “Start Advisor application,” sign in with your existing Portal credentials and complete the Advisor questions. You can return to your member account to check your membership status.
