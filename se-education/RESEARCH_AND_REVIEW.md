# SE Education — research, teaching standards and release review
Research review: 2026-11-10 · Status: staged curriculum drafts; not yet approved or deployed.

## Research benchmarks
- **National Coalition for Sexual Freedom, Educational Outreach** (2026): https://ncsfreedom.org/educational-outreach-overview/ — consent education, organizer outreach, teaching materials and risk literacy. Review each asset’s reuse license; do not copy the scripts or branding.
- **NCSF, Why Sexual Choking Is Not as Safe as You Think** (2026-09-02): https://ncsfreedom.org/2026/09/02/why-sexual-choking-is-not-as-safe-as-you-think-a-call-to-action-from-sex-researchers-and-kink-educators/ — an argument for stronger risk education and organizational responsibility. SE teaches hazards and safer alternatives but **not strangulation technique**.
- **NCSF, Choking Survey Report** (2026-09-01): https://ncsfreedom.org/2026/09/01/ncsfs-choking-survey-report/ — research context for student misconceptions and risk planning; survey findings should not be presented as prevalence estimates for SE members.
- **Wicked Grounds, 2026 class roster**: https://wickedgrounds.com/2026/08/ — benchmark for separate beginner/advanced negotiation classes, educator-led workshops, workbook offerings and accessible online instruction. SE scripts are original.
- **Wicked Grounds, class descriptions**: https://scene.events/?type=class — benchmark for publishing exact format, dates, requirements and accessibility. No permission to reproduce someone else’s course.
- **NCSF, Consent Counts**: https://ncsfreedom.org/key-programs-2/consent-counts/ — foundational consent and negotiation reference.

## Explicit program scope
- Standalone rope-tying courses, fire-play instruction, choking technique and a standalone first-aid class are **not** in the initial twelve-course offering.
- **Impact Play Foundations** includes equipment inspection, risk awareness, negotiation, educator demonstration on inert targets and follow-up, plus a short nonpractical restraint/neck-pressure hazard primer.
- Emergency escalation is taught briefly where relevant, without claiming medical certification or providing unsafe intervention instructions.
- The twelve imported course seeds and instructor run-of-show documents are development drafts. They require a named qualified instructor, SELC content review, founder approval and an event-linked release.

## Required preclass review for EACH of twelve courses
1. Assign a trained and appropriately qualified educator; identify demonstrations that need an assistant or specialized review.
2. Read aloud every scripted claim and check that it is clear, accurate, non-coercive and suited to Central Illinois members.
3. Check licensing, source dates and any required permission for external diagrams, slides, handouts, photographs or videos.
4. Match each minute on the run-of-show to actual activities and provide a time buffer.
5. Rehearse the consent and emergency stop language; avoid forcing disclosures or live participation.
6. Confirm venue access, class age policy, accessibility, evacuation, handwashing, cleaning, tools and attendance cap.
7. Validate each multiple-choice answer and its rationale; check for ambiguity and offensive or stigmatizing wording.
8. Test one approved attendee, one waitlisted attendee, another member, one Educator, SELC and Founder against the same course.
9. Confirm no public response or source map contains instructor scripts, student identity lists or quiz answer keys.
10. Publish only the Founder-approved version and associate Workshop event with immutable `classVersion`.

## Site workflow
**Portal:** account, RSVP, payment, check-in, enrollment, quiz record and completion status.
**SE Education:** public catalog; logged-in, enrolled student modules; saved progress; quizzes and completion check.
**SELC Headquarters:** educator proposals, reviews, Founder-controlled publication and prepared-draft import.
**Educator Studio (Portal):** visual lesson and quiz editing, module timing, slide bullets, private speaking notes, source list, presentation mode and printable instructor plan.
**Hub:** education discovery and events.

## Remaining human acceptance
A passing software test does not validate technical skills or medical correctness. Before production, conduct live role-based user acceptance tests and instructor rehearsal; do not publish detailed course content solely because code CI is green.
