WHAT WE DO / 01

KEEP IT
WORKING.

We are an internal leadership team, not another club or a separate event organizer.

Our job is straightforward: make Sanctum Eros easier to attend, safer to navigate, and more reliable to operate.

We help review membership applications, manage RSVPs, coordinate staffing, prepare events, answer member questions, and handle safety concerns through defined procedures. The Founder retains overall organizational responsibility.

Our focus.Upcoming events, member support, practical decisions, and good follow-through.

THE PRIORITIES / 02

SHOW UP.
DO THE WORK.

Everything here supports events, our members, or the people responsible for the space.

01 — EVENTS

Good events, consistently.

Dungeon Nights, socials and workshops planned around what we can responsibly host, not promises of an endless calendar.

02 — MEMBER SUPPORT

Clear answers.

03 — SAFETY

Accountability in practice.

Consent, privacy, staffed events, incident reporting and documented follow-up when something goes wrong.
