WHY WE EXIST / 01

MORE THAN
A PLACE TO
PLAY.

A community needs more than a calendar. It needs people willing to build something meaningful together.

Sanctum Eros is a private kink organization built around connection, education, consent, and the freedom to explore without pressure.

The Sanctum Eros Leadership Committee brings trusted community voices into that work. Together, we help develop classes, improve gatherings, welcome new ideas, listen to feedback, and care for the spaces and relationships that make this community possible.

We're not a separate club, and we're not here to dictate anyone's interests. We're part of Sanctum Eros—people working to make the organization more thoughtful, inclusive, and sustainable.

Everyone has something to contribute.Whether you help in person or from behind a screen, what matters is showing up with care and consistency.

OWN WALLS.

Connecting independent kink organizations through responsible collaboration—not competition or automatic access to private information.

The Community Leaders network connects owners and authorized organizers of other kink and BDSM organizations with SELC.

Apply to exchange education resources, discuss event operations and volunteer practices, collaborate on workshops, and learn from other organizers' experience. Accepted leaders can participate in a private discussion space after their identity and organization are verified.

Cross-organization safety communication is narrowly controlled. Neither approval nor partnership grants access to Sanctum Eros membership databases, confidential investigations, personal identity documents, or private venue addresses. Safety summaries require independent review and Founder authorization.

GOOD TO KNOW / 05

QUESTIONS?
GOOD.

Is SELC separate from Sanctum Eros?

No. SELC is Sanctum Eros's Leadership Committee, helping guide education, member experiences, and community initiatives.

Can I help without coming to every event?

Absolutely. Two Representative seats are primarily online, and every member can contribute remotely when the work allows it.

Do I have to be a kink educator or an expert?

No. Good judgment, reliability, care for others, curiosity, and willingness to learn are valuable contributions too.

How do I apply to SELC?

Become an approved Sanctum Eros member in good standing, sign in with your Portal member account, and submit a separate SELC Representative application. If the Founder accepts you, complete the required SELC certification before your committee appointment or voting privileges activate. Attending events and holding a paid supporter membership are not prerequisites.

Can leaders of other kink organizations collaborate with SELC?

Yes. Community Leaders is an independently approved network for owners and authorized organizers. It does not require SE membership and does not confer SELC seats, votes, Staff access, or internal IA permissions. Visit Community Leaders to apply.

What about safety and personal privacy?

SELC is committed to respectful conduct, consent, and thoughtful handling of concerns. Private membership and safety information is available only to people authorized to help with it.
