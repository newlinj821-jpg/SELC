WHY WE EXIST / 01

MORE THAN
A PLACE TO
PLAY.

A community needs more than a calendar. It needs people willing to build something meaningful together.

Sanctum Eros is a kink organization built around connection, education, consent, and the freedom to explore without pressure. SELC provides its leadership and community-development structure.

Founded to strengthen the way Sanctum Eros is managed and operated, SELC also works beyond its own events. We develop education, share practical resources, collaborate with community organizers, support better experiences, and create opportunities for people to contribute.

SELC is part of Sanctum Eros, not a separate club. Its responsibilities begin with the organization, but its commitment to education, connection, and community resources reaches beyond our own walls.

Everyone has something to contribute.Whether you help in person or from behind a screen, what matters is showing up with care and consistency.

Connecting independent kink organizations through responsible collaboration—not competition or automatic access to private information.

The Community Leaders network connects owners and authorized organizers of other kink and BDSM organizations with SELC.

Apply to exchange education resources, discuss event operations and volunteer practices, collaborate on workshops, and learn from other organizers' experience. Accepted leaders can participate in a private discussion space after their identity and organization are verified.

GOOD TO KNOW / 05

QUESTIONS?
GOOD.

Is SELC separate from Sanctum Eros?

No. SELC was founded to improve how Sanctum Eros is managed and operated. It also develops education, resources, and community initiatives that can benefit people and organizations beyond Sanctum Eros.

Can I help without coming to every event?

Absolutely. Participation can be in person or online, depending on the work. Members choose how to describe their participation on their public profiles.

Do I have to be a kink educator or an expert?

No. Good judgment, reliability, care for others, curiosity, and willingness to learn are valuable contributions too.

How do I apply to SELC?

Become an approved Sanctum Eros member in good standing, sign in with your Portal member account, and apply for an Advisor or Representative position through SELC. If your application is accepted, complete the required SELC certification before your committee role or voting privileges activate. Attending events and holding a paid supporter membership are not prerequisites.

Can leaders of other kink organizations collaborate with SELC?

Yes. Community Leaders is an independently approved network for owners and authorized organizers. It does not require SE membership and does not confer SELC seats, votes, Staff access, or internal IA permissions. Visit Community Leaders to apply.

What about safety and personal privacy?

SELC supports consent education, respectful conduct, and responsible community safety work. Access to private membership and incident information is limited to authorized people.
