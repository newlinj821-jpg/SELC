01 / OUR PURPOSE

Not a boardroom.
A community at work.

SELC exists to help Sanctum Eros grow as a community-led kink organization. Our representatives contribute their experience, ideas, and time toward education, outreach, member support, events, and improvements to the spaces we share.

Every representative can participate across all areas of the committee. Focus areas reflect individual interests—not restrictions, ranks, or rigid departments.

✧Three representative seats anticipate both venue and online participation. Two are primarily online, with optional in-person involvement.
