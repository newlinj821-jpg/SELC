THE TEAM BEHIND SANCTUM EROS

THE PEOPLE
BEHIND SANCTUM EROS.

SELC is the internal leadership committee of Sanctum Eros. We review membership applications, coordinate Dungeon Nights and socials, support event staff, respond to member questions, and follow up on safety concerns. We work with the people attending our events, not with an outside organizer network.

WHAT WE DO / 01

LEADERSHIP WITH
A PURPOSE.

Our responsibility is to keep Sanctum Eros organized, responsive and accountable.

Members deserve clear expectations, attentive leadership, and events that run well from start to finish.

Representatives process membership and event work; Coordinators oversee operations and administrative decisions; Advisors offer informed input on member feedback, event improvements, and eligible committee proposals. The Founder retains overall organizational responsibility. Staff and Dungeon Monitors carry out their separate event duties.

One accountable team.Members, staff and SELC use the Sanctum Eros Portal for shared attendance, membership and operational records. Sensitive reporting follows restricted access rules.

THE PRIORITIES / 02

WHAT WE
ACTUALLY DO.

Our remit is simple: events, membership, staffing, communication and safety.

01 — EVENTS

Reliable event operations

Plan realistic Dungeon Nights, socials and workshops. Prepare staffing and check-in, coordinate RSVPs, and handle changes before they become problems.

02 — MEMBER SUPPORT

Real member support

Review complete applications, ask for clarification, answer event questions and reply to member-authorized messages in one system.

03 — SAFETY

Appropriate accountability

Maintain consent and privacy expectations, protect incident records, and make sure concerns reach authorized decision-makers.

OUR TEAM / 03

THE PEOPLE
DOING THE WORK.

SELC has two Coordinators, four Representatives and two Advisors, alongside the Founder. Each position carries defined responsibilities and limits. No title substitutes for certification or event-staff authority.

FOUNDER / DIRECTOR

Eros

Founder of SELC and owner of Sanctum Eros.

COMMITTEE LEADERSHIP

Coordinators

Two Coordinators oversee SE administration, distribute work, review management decisions and provide continuity for events and staffing.

EVENTS & OPERATIONS

Representatives

Four Representatives process routine membership applications, manage approved event operations, assist members and keep day-to-day work moving.

FEEDBACK & SUPPORT

Advisors

Two Advisors review appropriately shared member feedback, offer perspectives on event improvements and take part in internal proposals. They cannot independently approve members, access restricted incident files or issue bans.

AN OPPORTUNITY TO CONTRIBUTE / 04

GOOD INPUT.
BETTER EVENTS.

You don't have to run an event or make enforcement decisions to contribute to how Sanctum Eros operates.

The Advisor position is for members who listen carefully, take feedback seriously, and have constructive ideas for improving the member experience. Advisors can participate in private committee discussions, review communications explicitly shared with SELC, and vote on eligible proposals.

There are two appointed Advisor positions. Applicants must hold approved SE membership. Appointment and role-specific certification are required before SELC access begins.

ADVISOR ROLE & APPLICATION ↗BECOME AN SE MEMBER →
ADVISORS MAY
  • Read opted-in messages and event feedback
  • Suggest practical improvements to events and operations
  • Participate in internal proposals and SELC private chat
  • Escalate safety concerns to authorized leadership
Boundaries matter.

Advisors cannot approve membership, change someone’s venue access, or independently make safety-enforcement decisions.
