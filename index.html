WHAT WE DO / 01

LEADERSHIP WITH
A PURPOSE.

We help turn ideas into events, welcome new members and look after the community.

Much of our work happens between events: reading applications, planning the next gathering and talking with members.

Representatives manage authorized applications, RSVPs and routine event work. Coordinators assign and oversee administrative responsibilities. Advisors contribute perspective on member feedback and possible improvements without exercising independent approval or enforcement authority. The Founder/Director is responsible for organizational direction and accountability. Event Staff and Dungeon Monitors operate under their own training and duties.

Shared records. Clear responsibilities.Our Member Portal keeps membership, attendance, event and support records in one place. Access to applications, messages and safety reports depends on each person’s authorized role. Official Dungeon Rules and event requirements are published there; SELC oversees only its authorized operational work.

FOUNDER / DIRECTOR

Eros

Responsible for organizational direction and accountability for SELC, within its established governance process.

COMMITTEE LEADERSHIP

Coordinators

Two Coordinators organize the committee’s workload, oversee authorized administrative decisions and help ensure event teams have what they need. They operate within the authority assigned to them.

EVENTS & OPERATIONS

Representatives

Four Representatives review member applications, coordinate RSVPs and event operations, answer member questions and handle the day-to-day tasks that keep SE running.

FEEDBACK & SUPPORT

Advisors

Two Advisors read member feedback that has been shared with SELC, suggest practical improvements, participate in eligible committee proposals and use the private SELC chat. They cannot approve applications, access restricted incident records or issue bans.

AN OPPORTUNITY TO CONTRIBUTE / 04

GOOD INPUT.
BETTER EVENTS.

Thoughtful feedback matters. Advisors give SE members another way to contribute without taking on venue administration or enforcement.

An Advisor listens to members, notices where the experience can improve and brings ideas to the committee for consideration. This is a working volunteer role: respectful discussion, reasonable confidentiality and constructive follow-through are expected.

SELC appoints up to two Advisors. To apply, you need an approved, active SE membership, an existing Portal login and a completed Advisor application. Founder approval and Advisor certification are required before the appointment becomes active.

ADVISOR ROLE & APPLICATION ↗BECOME AN SE MEMBER →
ADVISORS MAY
  • Read opted-in messages and event feedback
  • Suggest practical improvements to events and operations
  • Participate in internal proposals and SELC private chat
  • Escalate safety concerns to authorized leadership
Boundaries matter.

Advisors cannot approve membership, change someone’s venue access, or independently make safety-enforcement decisions.

HOW WE WORK / 05

FROM A QUESTION
TO A RESPONSE.

Members should know where to go, what happens next and who can make a decision.

01 / SEND

Start in the Portal

Submit a membership application, event question, suggestion or authorized message through Sanctum Eros.

02 / REVIEW

The right people review it

Representatives and Coordinators handle authorized operational work. Advisors may contribute only where member consent and role permissions permit.

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

A response or decision

Updates stay with the original request. Sensitive reports use restricted procedures rather than public chat or informal discussion.

Need assistance?

Members can use the Portal to contact Sanctum Eros. For urgent concerns during an event, speak directly with event staff or a Dungeon Monitor.
