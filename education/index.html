SELC / Community education

KNOWLEDGE IS A COMMUNITY RESOURCE.

Education is a central responsibility of the Sanctum Eros Leadership Committee. We want more than occasional demonstrations: practical classes, accessible conversations, honest risk-awareness, and room for people to ask questions.

01 / Our approach

TEACH THE WHY.

Good education does not begin with an impressive title. It begins with a willingness to explain what you know, acknowledge what you do not, and give learners information they can use. Classes should discuss consent, negotiation, risks, technique, stopping points, and aftercare where applicable—not just how a demonstration looks. Free self-guided resources remain on the Sanctum Eros Hub; SELC coordinates people, proposals, and teaching support.

Our Community Educators may teach introductions, discussion sessions, hands-on skills, specialty techniques, relationship topics, safety planning, accessibility, and many other subjects relevant to kink. Different teaching styles belong here. No one is required to participate in an exercise or to disclose personal experiences to attend.

SELC helps educators refine ideas, develop outlines, identify supplies, anticipate accessibility needs, and arrange mentorship. Eros gives final approval before an activity becomes a scheduled event. Public resources may be available to adults 18+; classes and socials are 18+ even when held at the dungeon; party events require 21+.

02 / Become an educator

BRING WHAT YOU KNOW.

Apply thoughtfully

Tell SELC what you would teach, your practical experience, your teaching approach, how you handle consent and risk, and what accessibility means in your planning.

Build a real class

Work with the committee on objectives, outlines, supplies, consent procedures, and mentoring. Feedback and revisions are a normal part of development.

Teach with support

Founder approval, event logistics, appropriate staff or DM coverage, and participant expectations are settled before a class appears on the calendar.

Applications and workshop plans are separate. Approval as a Community Educator does not automatically authorize every lesson or demonstrate every technique.

03 / What would you like to learn?

REQUEST A CLASS.

You do not need to be an educator to suggest a topic. If you want a beginner-friendly introduction, a discussion about community practice, or a specific skill taught more thoroughly, bring the idea to SELC. We can connect suggestions with educators, look for appropriate resources, and develop programming when there is interest and capacity.

We also welcome experienced people who want guidance before teaching for the first time. Being technically capable and being a capable instructor are different skills; both deserve support.
