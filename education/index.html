01 / Our approach

PRACTICAL AND CONSENT-FOCUSED.

Classes emphasize informed consent, clear negotiation, risk awareness, boundaries and when to stop. Participation in demonstrations or exercises is voluntary, and an educational session never replaces a venue-specific safety orientation. You can explore Sanctum Eros free education resources without registering for a workshop.

SELC coordinates workshop logistics and ensures that proposed classes fit the venue and available staffing. Courses are announced only when there is a confirmed event.

02 / Behind the session

FROM PROPOSAL TO CLASS.

SELC coordinates the people and practical requirements for a workshop. The Hub houses the learning resources; the Portal manages the event and attendance record.

Propose a topic

An educator or demonstrator describes the purpose, intended audience, experience required, and any equipment or assistance needed. A proposal is not an automatic booking.

Review the plan

Authorized organizers confirm the presenter, room suitability, staffing, accessibility needs, safety constraints, and what attendees should know beforehand.

Publish and prepare

A workshop becomes available only when approved and scheduled. The event listing carries its actual fee, eligibility, capacity, and RSVP details.

03 / Participation

FIND THE RIGHT SESSION.

Check the calendar

See which workshops are actually scheduled.

Check admission and eligibility

Some workshops may be public; others are members-only. Fees, age requirements, capacity, prerequisites, and orientation rules belong to the current event listing.

RSVP through the Portal

Submit the event RSVP, receive confirmation and follow the stated payment and attendance instructions. Attending a workshop does not waive the separate Dungeon Night orientation or its first-time $10 fee.
